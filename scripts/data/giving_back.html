WISH UPON A TEEN

Donations from Berri help to fund the Design My Room®, Wish Upon a Teen's premier program. Donations provide games, music, arts and crafts, books, picture frames, blankets and decals for the hospital rooms of teens battling cancer. As of 2020, Berri Brands has helped to fund over 530 Design My Rooms for childhood cancer patients.
